Stackdiff privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Stackdiff is a browser extension made by Nino Ventures. It shows the theme and apps of the Shopify store in your current tab. This page explains exactly what it does with data. Short version: what you check stays in your browser, and the only thing that ever leaves it is your Pro license key, which goes to our payment provider to confirm it is valid.

What the extension reads

Only when you click the Stackdiff icon, and only on the tab you clicked it on, Stackdiff reads that page's HTML, the addresses of the scripts and files the page loaded, and the public window.Shopify object the store puts on its own page. It uses this to recognise the theme and the apps. It does not read form fields, passwords, cookies or anything you typed. It does not run on other tabs and does not run in the background.

If you have Pro and press "Read product count and prices", the extension asks that store for its public product feed (/products.json), at most 4 requests. That request goes from your browser to the store, the same way the store's own pages load it.

What it stores

For each store you check, Stackdiff saves the store's domain, name, theme, the list of apps and pixels it found, and the date, in your browser's local extension storage. It keeps up to 500 stores. This is how it can tell you what changed next time. If you have Pro, it also stores your license key and an activation ID. You can delete the history at any time with "Clear history" in the History tab. Uninstalling the extension removes everything.

What it sends

  • Nothing about the pages or stores you check. There is no Stackdiff server, no analytics, no tracking, no ads and no third-party scripts.
  • When you activate Pro, and about once a day after that, the extension sends your license key, an activation ID and the extension version to Polar (api.polar.sh) to check that the key is valid. Polar is our merchant of record and handles payments under its own privacy policy.

We don't sell or share data, and we don't use it for anything other than the extension's single purpose. The extension's use of data follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Buying Pro

When you buy Pro, Polar processes your payment and gives us your name, email address and order details so we can deliver the key and help you if something goes wrong. See our general privacy policy.

Permissions

  • activeTab: read the tab you clicked the icon on, only at that moment.
  • scripting: run the detection code in that tab.
  • storage: keep your history and license key on your computer.

Stackdiff asks for no access to all websites, no browsing history and no downloads permission.
