Stackdiff · Browser extension · Nino Ventures

See what a Shopify store runs, and what it changed.

Click the icon on any Shopify store and Stackdiff lists its theme and the apps it recognises. It remembers each store you check, so next time you see which apps were added or removed.

Coming to the Chrome Web Store soon. The free version will stay free.

Free

  • Theme name, version, the theme it's based on, and whether it's from the Theme Store or custom.
  • Apps matched against 130+ signatures: Klaviyo, Judge.me, Yotpo, ReCharge, Gorgias, Okendo, Attentive, Rebuy and more, grouped by what they do.
  • Headless storefronts are labelled as headless instead of guessed.
  • Copy a short summary to paste in your notes.

Pro: $4 a month

  • What changed since your last visit: apps added, apps removed, theme switched or updated.
  • App embeds that aren't in our list yet, by their handle.
  • Ad and analytics pixels: Meta, TikTok, Google Ads, GA4, GTM, Pinterest and others.
  • Product count, vendors, price range and newest product date from the store's public product feed.
  • Your full history of checked stores, exportable as CSV.

Pro checkout opens when the extension is live. One key works on three browsers. Cancel any time from your Polar customer portal.

Need it for hundreds of stores?

The extension checks one store at a time. For a list of domains, use the Shopify App & Theme Detector on Apify, which runs the same detection in bulk and can watch stores for app installs and removals.

Privacy

What you check stays in your browser. There are no analytics and no Stackdiff server. Only your Pro key is sent to Polar to check that it's valid. Details in the privacy policy.
