When Filing Alerts is the right buy

  • You want Telegram, not another inbox folder.
  • You watch a short list (≤25 tickers), not the whole market.
  • You will open the SEC link yourself — we do not paraphrase filings.
  • You do not need FDA/EPA streams, full-text search, or AI digests.

When to pay more instead

  • Gunpowder ($30/mo) if you want AI summaries and multi-channel delivery across many agencies — their pricing.
  • Earnings Feed Pro ($10–15/mo) if you want email alerts plus search/export/API on a research site — their pricing.
  • BamSEC / Finviz Elite / sec-api.io if you need a filing reader, screener-tied alerts, or a developer API (see independent roundups such as Dombey’s 2026 comparison).
