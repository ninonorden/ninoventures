Shopify app detector without guesswork

People still paste a store URL into a random “tech looker” and hope the result is right. The reliable path is shorter: read what the storefront actually loads, match it against known app signatures, and remember what you saw last time.

What you can detect from the page

  • Theme name, version, and whether it is a Theme Store theme or custom.
  • Apps that inject scripts or liquid snippets with stable handles: Klaviyo, Judge.me, Yotpo, ReCharge, Gorgias, Okendo, Attentive, Rebuy and about 130 others.
  • Headless storefronts (Hydrogen and friends) so you do not invent a theme name.
  • Ad and analytics pixels when you want the paid picture: Meta, TikTok, GA4, GTM, Pinterest.

One store: use a browser extension

Stackdiff is our extension for that. Click the icon on any Shopify store. Free tells you theme and matched apps. Pro ($4/month) keeps history so you see apps added or removed since your last visit, plus pixels and a CSV export. Hundreds of stores: run it in bulk

The extension is for the store you have open. For a spreadsheet of domains, use the Shopify App & Theme Detector on Apify. Same signatures, pay per store, optional watch for installs and removals. There is also a walkthrough on building a lead list from a specific app.

What not to trust

BuiltWith-style crawl databases go stale. Page-source greps that only look for one string miss embeds and app blocks. If you care about a competitor stack this week, detect it this week.
